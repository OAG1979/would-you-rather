WOULD YOU RATHER

The Food Bracket

Foods go head to head. You pick which one you'd rather eat, and the winner slays the loser with a sword. Keep going until only one food is left, then see the leaderboard.

Tap a food to pick it. Undo takes back your last pick. Music starts when you pick a mode. Every mode saves your spot, and Restart starts fresh.

Music not working? Tap the music button in the corner to turn it off, then tap it again to turn it back on.
